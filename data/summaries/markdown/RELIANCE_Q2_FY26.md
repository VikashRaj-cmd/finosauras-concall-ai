# Reliance Industries Ltd — Q2 FY26 Earnings Call Summary

## TL;DR
- Reliance delivered an exceptionally strong Q2 FY26, achieving highest-ever consolidated EBITDA (Rs.62,409 Cr, +24% YoY) and PAT (close to Rs.30,000 Cr, +34% YoY), primarily driven by O2C and Jio Platforms.
- O2C segment 'led the charge' with a 69% increase in EBITDA, benefiting from unprecedented tightness in energy markets, operational excellence, and advantageous crude sourcing despite elevated costs.
- Jio Platforms continued strong growth with 542 million subscribers (300 million 5G), ARPU of Rs.218.8 (3.5% YoY growth without tariff hikes), and established market leadership in fixed wireless services.
- Reliance Retail's EBITDA was strategically lower (down 10%) due to significant investments in building a scalable digital commerce platform, which is showing early progress with online grocery B2C up 42%.
- New Energy projects are on track for commissioning, with solar module capacity at 6+ GW (targeting 20 GW early next year) and battery cell capacity ramping up for FY2028 revenue contribution.

## Management Commentary
- Consolidated performance was an 'exceptionally strong quarter in a turbulent world,' and management is 'mighty pleased with the performance.'
- O2C 'led the charge,' 'benefited from the unprecedented tightness' in energy markets, and demonstrated 'operational excellence,' vindicating a 'structurally positive view about this business.'
- Jio Platforms completed the 'largest IPO,' became the 'second largest company by market cap on the first day,' and established 'market leadership' in the home segment as the 'world's largest fixed wireless service.'
- Reliance Retail's lower EBITDA is part of an 'unchanged strategy' to build an online presence and scalable digital commerce platform, with 'cash flow from our store business, in a way, is funding digital commerce growth.'
- The company is a 'full stack tech company serving customers, homes, and businesses at nation scale,' with 'unmatched distribution capabilities' and a 'culture of innovation.'
- FMCG delivered 'strong revenue growth' in Q2 and H1, with positive traction across daily essentials, beverages, home & personal care, and packaged foods.
- JioStar exited the quarter with 'strong MAU growth' and is 'very bullish about the rest of the financial year.'
- Petrochemicals had an 'absolutely fantastic quarter' and half year, 'still in the midst of that sweet spot,' due to steady ethane prices and advantageous feedstock sourcing.

## Guidance & Outlook
- Consolidated first half of FY 2027 performance is tracking annualized EBITDA 'well over Rs.80,000 Crores.'
- New Energy earnings contribution is 'definitely starting in FY 2028,' with solar module capacity reaching 20 GW early next year and solar cell capacity 20 GW first half next year. BESS facility to be commissioned in the next half year, battery cell late next year.
- Jio sees 'visibility of high teen growth,' expects fixed wireless to 'ramp up in the coming quarters,' and capex has 'started tapering down' as 5G deployment is largely complete.
- Reliance Retail expects the benefit of building the digital commerce platform to be seen through 'FY 2028 and FY 2029' when scale translates into value, with investments expected to be accretive from next year and a target to double EBITDA in two years.
- O2C 'visibility is getting better' on fundamentally changed markets, with Rs.80,000 Crores on the O2C basis for the year expected, and cracks expected to remain elevated.
- The 44 megawatt data center will be done in the next four to six months, with plans for another 120-megawatt data center.
- The company is 'absolutely keen' on IPL bidding, but will address objectively once the tender comes out, keeping profitable growth and disciplined bidding in mind.

## Segment Performance
- **Consolidated**: Revenues up 28%, EBITDA up 24% (Rs.62,409 Crores, highest ever), PAT up 34% (close to Rs.30,000 Crores, all-time high), primarily driven by O2C and Jio. Capex almost flat. Debt numbers (September 2026) about 250, about 0.47 (ratio).
- **Oil to Chemicals (O2C)**: EBITDA up 68-69% (Rs.25,203 Crores), 'led the charge,' benefiting from tight fuel markets, downstream cracks, and advantageous fuel sourcing. Achieved record crude runs despite disruptions. Switched product placement to East, West, South African, LATAM, and EU markets for best netback. Doubled LPG production. Coker, platformer, and FCC operated at very high levels. Polyester chain margins up 33%.
- **Jio Platforms (JPL)**: PAT up 14% (Rs.8,387 Crores). Total subscriber base 542 million (36 million net adds over last 12 months), 300 million 5G subscribers. 30 million fixed connections (half FWA), 15.1 million fixed wireless service connections (over 78% market share). Average data consumption 44.2 GB per user per month (14% growth YoY). ARPU Rs.218.8 (3.5% growth YoY without tariff increases). Revenue from operations Rs.40,475 Crores (11.4% YoY). EBITDA Rs.21,879 Crores (16.6% YoY). RJIL EBITDA margin 58.1% (improved almost 200 basis points). Net addition of subscribers during the quarter: 9.1 million. Data traffic: 71.3 billion GB.
- **Reliance Retail (RRVL)**: Revenue increase about 6%. EBITDA about 10% lower (Rs.6,228 Crores). PAT Rs.2,611 Crores (lower) due to strategic digital commerce transition. Online grocery B2C up 42%. Registered customer base crossed 400 million (up 10% YoY). Transactions grew 7%. Opened 303 new stores, shut 500 unviable stores. EBITDA margin 7.8%. One-time exceptional charge of Rs.108 Crores on account of 7-eleven. Retail area 77.5 million square feet. B2B side has almost 4 million customers. Like-for-Like (LFL) growth positive. Omni-channel network of 5000+ stores across 1000+ cities. Ajio Rush orders grew 36% YoY. Share of own brands up 710 basis points. Consumer electronics LFL growth 8%, distribution business growth 20%. Fashion and lifestyle LFL growth 3%.
- **Oil and Gas**: Almost flat, 2% lower. Production 25 million standard cubic meters. KG-D6 natural decline offset by elevated oil and gas prices. CBM production continues uptrend.
- **Digital Services**: 18% higher at about Rs.22,200 Crores.
- **FMCG**: Q2 Revenue Rs.8,266 Crores (1.6 times YoY growth). H1 Revenue Rs.16,900 Crores (1.8 times last year's first half). Daily Essentials Revenue Rs.7,000 Crores (1.7 times YoY). Beverages H1 Revenue Rs.5,250 Crores (90% of full year sales of 2026 in six months), led by Campa (number three NRTD player). Entered dairy (Bombay Creamery) and biscuits. Expanding internationally in Australia, Kenya, African markets, GCC, and Southeast Asia. Investing in manufacturing capabilities and integrated food and beverage parks.
- **JioStar Digital Platform**: Exited the quarter with 440 million MAU (10% YoY growth). Conversational discovery feature engagement grew 75% QoQ. Sports viewership very strong, especially on CTV, with monetization improving towards quarter-end. Unscripted entertainment (Bigg Boss, Khatron Ke Khiladi) showed tremendous strength. Original shows performing well. Box office releases (Drishyam 3) achieved significant milestones. EBITDA margins continue to be industry leading. Launched in UK, Canada, Singapore.
- **Reliance Brands Limited (RBL)**: Crossed the milestone of 1000+ stores. Market leader with the biggest collection of international brands and store footprint. Ajio LUXE has almost 1,040 brands. Launched Fabletics, signed exclusive partnership with SKIMS, launched Hugo Boss and sub-brand Hugo, launched Madden NYC.
- **New Energy**: Solar module capacity is now 6+ gigawatt. Solar cell capacity nearing 5-gigawatt. BESS facility (pack and container) capacity up to 40-gigawatt hour. Battery cell capacity around 35-gigawatt to 40-gigawatt hour. Progressing on ingot, wafer, polysilicon, and glass manufacturing.
- **Data Center**: 44 megawatt data center in process, to be done in next four to six months. Planning another 120-megawatt data center.

## Notable Q&A
### Regarding 11.8 million barrels/day refinery impact in Q2, with 4-5 million barrels still affected (Russia/Middle East), and geopolitics not changing, is 5 million barrels 6-9 months away from normalization or longer?
- **Answer**: The 11.8 million barrels impacted Middle East refineries at different points, which are now coming up. The 5 million barrels refers to those yet to come up in the Middle East and Russia (40% of capacity). Nobody knows the resolution timeline; uncertainty is very high, and the situation is not expected to change immediately.
- **Asked by**: Probal Sen

### With improved Q3 Middle East flows but inherent risk/uncertainty, how is the sourcing mix positioned, and is there a realignment back to Middle East flows?
- **Answer**: The company swung dramatically between East of Suez and West of Suez in Q2 month-to-month and will continue to do so, looking for opportunistic crude sources for the next quarter.
- **Asked by**: Probal Sen

### For O2C profit growth, did it come from refineries, petrochemicals, or additional ethane carriers, given refinery crack spreads were not dramatically up (except gasoline) and freight costs were higher, but petrochemical margins seemed better?
- **Answer**: It was a combined effect. Petrochemicals definitely contributed. Refining also contributed due to diesel price increase, mitigation of freight impact by a significant TC fleet, gasifier margin uplift, and product premiums.
- **Asked by**: Jal Irani

### Is there structural demand destruction, especially in China (EVs, LNG as fuel), leading to permanently lower refining margins after current highs?
- **Answer**: China's move to EVs and gas-based trucks is structural. However, demand in the rest of the world is expected to rise, offsetting Chinese demand (2.5-2.6 million barrels reduction in 2026, rebounding to 2-2.3 million barrels in 2027). Market memory of past events and current attacks means normalization of insurance and freight is not expected quickly.
- **Asked by**: Jal Irani

### Can we get an update on the commissioning of new energy lines?
- **Answer**: Well on target. Solar module capacity is 6+ GW now, 20 GW early next year. Solar cell nearing 5 GW, 20 GW first half next year. Progressing on ingot, wafer, polysilicon, glass. BESS facility (pack/container) up to 40 GWh in next half year. Cell late next year (35-40 GWh).
- **Asked by**: Sanjay Mookim

### On retail, with investments impacting margins, is there a timeframe for investment completion and expected EBITDA margin flow? Also, where will incremental capex go in the next 12 months (data center, IPL renewal)?
- **Answer**: Competition is intense, investing in infrastructure/customer acquisition/margins, online has lower inherent margins, benefits in absolute EBITDA from next year, confident in doubling EBITDA in two years. 44MW data center in 4-6 months, planning 120MW more, building infra for hyperscalers.
- **Asked by**: Sachin Salgaonkar

### Are dark stores mainly in urban areas or also in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities?
- **Answer**: More in bigger cities to meet 15-20 minute delivery, but not exclusively.
- **Asked by**: Sachin Salgaonkar

### Are you keen on IPL bidding?
- **Answer**: Absolutely yes, it's a core strategic agenda, but depends on price, tender, and profitable growth/disciplined bidding philosophy.
- **Asked by**: Sachin Salgaonkar

### Retail online grocery growth (46%) is underwhelming; are you still confident of doubling EBITDA in two years?
- **Answer**: Not changing the target, making investments this year, economics improve next year. Online platform growth (42% YoY) is measured, leveraging 400M customers and 20,000 stores, confident in EBITDA growth post-investment phase.
- **Asked by**: Vikash Jain

### O2C spreads capture improved this quarter; will it continue to improve with new vessels?
- **Answer**: Ethane benefit will increase with vessels; previous quarter had an LPG control order issue which is milder this quarter, helping volumes.
- **Asked by**: Vikash Jain

## Risks & Concerns
- The quarter occurred in a 'turbulent world and turbulence in macro, turbulence because of energy disruption.'
- O2C performance was achieved 'on the back of significantly elevated OpEx, the kind of crude premiums that we saw, the kind of surge in freight rates, insurance costs.'
- Structural supply constraints continue in O2C, with crude oil disruptions (Saudi Arabia pipeline hit) and refining capacity impacts in Middle East and Russia.
- Prices have not been passed on in line with the international market, and the SAED levy is contributing to negative margins in marketing.
- Reliance Retail's EBITDA was lower due to a strategic decision to trade off 'short term earnings growth to build a very scalable digital commerce platform.'
- The festive season shifted entirely to Q3, impacting Q2 revenue growth for Retail.
- High inflation (two-year high), currency depreciation, monsoon deficit, and rising food prices are impacting purchasing power and discretionary demand.
- The quick commerce market is competitive with six players, leading to margin pressures in grocery, especially in metros.
- The Middle East situation caused an overhang on JioStar's sports monetization during the first part of the quarter.
- The petrochemical industry in Southeast Asia and Far East Asia struggled to get feed at exorbitant prices, with astronomical freight rates due to tanker shortages and high insurance charges.
- Naphtha flows from the Middle East dropped significantly, and Russian material disappeared from Asian markets due to refinery damage, creating serious material acquisition challenges.
- Global cracker utilization rates dropped to very low levels, and Middle East operating rates dropped significantly due to inability to evacuate products.
- Overcapacity in China is likely to emerge in the next two years as more ethylene capacities start.
- Natural decline in KG-D6 production.
- LNG offtake was impacted due to the Strait of Hormuz situation.
- Ceiling prices for oil and gas capped realized prices, creating a distortion between market and realized prices.
- Geopolitics are not expected to change immediately, leading to high uncertainty regarding refinery impacts and no quick normalization of insurance and freight costs.
