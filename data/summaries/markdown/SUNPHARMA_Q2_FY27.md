# Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd — Q2 FY27 Earnings Call Summary

## TL;DR
* Sales grew 11.8% YoY to INR 161,000 million.
* EBITDA margin declined to 29.3% due to prior year lenalidomide.
* India business grew 17.3% driven by volume and new launches.

## Management Commentary
* Ilumya US slowdown attributed to market and execution factors.
* Gross margin improved via product mix and currency tailwinds.
* Voluntary recall of 11 ophthalmic products for patient safety.

## Guidance & Outlook
* FY27 top-line growth remains high single-digit.
* R&D spend expected to ramp up in second half.
* Ilumya growth expected to normalize after psoriatic arthritis launch.
