# Tata Motors Ltd — Q2 FY27 Earnings Call Summary

## TL;DR
* Consolidated revenue up 34% YoY to Rs. 96,000 Cr.
* India volumes hit record 2 lakh units; EV share 43%.
* JLR faces China decline but strong UK and core brand growth.

## Management Commentary
* Commodity costs elevated in Q3, plateauing in Q4.
* EV demand significantly exceeds supply, causing low inventory.
* JLR resetting breakeven volumes to 300,000 units.

## Guidance & Outlook
* Net debt to close FY27 around Rs. 47,000 Cr.
* JLR FY27 EBIT expected in 2-3% range.
* Domestic capex to be 6-7% of revenue in FY27.

| Segment | Notes |
